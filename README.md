# SkillBridge — Refugee Talent to Opportunity

**ILO Mini-Hackathon 2026 | Interactive front-end prototype**

## Project summary
SkillBridge is a practical, community-driven pathway that helps skilled refugees move from underemployment to meaningful work. It addresses three connected barriers:

1. **Credential recognition and skills validation** — evidence-backed skills passports, practical assessment and bridging pathways.
2. **Legal, regulatory and financial access** — a guided readiness checklist that helps participants and trusted partners map work authorisation, regulated-profession requirements, payment access and employer onboarding.
3. **Local experience and professional networks** — skills-based matching, mentorship, micro-projects, fellowships and employer introductions.

The product is designed as a navigation and connection layer. It does not issue credentials, provide legal advice, grant work authorisation, open bank accounts or guarantee employment.

## Run the prototype
No installation or build tools are required.

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
3. Navigate through Overview, Talent profiles, Skills & credentials, Opportunities, Mentorship & networks and Impact tracker.

For best results, use Chrome, Edge or Firefox with an internet connection for the optional Google Fonts. The interface still works without the font connection.

## Demo data and privacy
All names, profile records, programme counts, match percentages and opportunities are illustrative. They are not real participant data, verified results, live jobs or confirmed partner commitments. Do not enter sensitive personal information into this static prototype.

A production deployment should use informed consent, data minimisation, role-based access, secure storage, retention limits, participant access/correction/deletion processes, and referral to qualified legal, licensing and financial partners. Work authorisation and professional licensing requirements vary by jurisdiction and must be confirmed by competent authorities.

- **SDG 8:** Decent work and economic growth through fair access to work and experience.
- **SDG 10:** Reduced inequalities by lowering structural barriers.
- **SDG 16:** Strong institutions through transparent processes, safeguards and trusted referrals.
- **SDG 17:** Partnerships among refugee-led groups, employers, assessors, training providers and service organisations.

## Suggested 90-second pitch
“Skilled refugees do not lack talent. Too often, systems cannot see or unlock it. A degree may be difficult to verify, work and payment requirements can be confusing, and employers ask for local experience that people have never had a fair chance to gain.

SkillBridge turns those barriers into a practical pathway. First, we help people build evidence-backed skills passports and connect them to qualified assessment or bridging options. Second, we make work-readiness steps visible, including work authorisation, regulated-profession requirements, payment access and employer onboarding. Third, we connect people to mentors, structured micro-projects, fellowships and skills-based opportunities.

Our innovation is the connected journey: not another job board, but a trusted bridge between talent, evidence, readiness and opportunity. We measure verified skills, readiness plans, employer engagement, quality placements and partner scalability. We will pilot with refugee-led organisations and trusted technical partners, learn from participants, and improve the model cohort by cohort.

SkillBridge helps turn brain waste into shared economic value. Your skills. Your recognition. Your opportunity.”
