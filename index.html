FIVE PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS

How we know the model is working

01Credential & skills verification

Share of participants with evidence-backed skills profiles or a recognised next-step plan.

Measure: verified competencies / bridging referrals
02Access & compliance readiness

Participants with a documented work-authorisation, regulated-work and payment access pathway.

Measure: completed readiness plans
03Practical & market fit

Alignment between participant capabilities, portfolios and employer role requirements.

Measure: interviews, projects and employer feedback
04Social & SDG impact

Progress toward decent work, skills development and reduced exclusion.

Measure: quality placements and participant-reported outcomes
05Sustainability & scalability

Ability to repeat the process with partners across sectors and locations.

Measure: partner retention, cost per pathway and cohort replication

All figures shown are illustrative prototype data, not verified programme results. A live deployment would use consent-based, privacy-protective monitoring.
